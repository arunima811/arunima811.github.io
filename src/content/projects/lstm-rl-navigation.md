## What it was

Research with Prof. Zoran Tiganj on how the brain perceives space while navigating. We built LSTM-based reinforcement learning models that simulate grid cells and place cells in a 2-D environment.

## What was different

Most models of this kind are trained on simulated rat trajectories. We used data from human test subjects instead.

## Methods

- Model-based RL: value iteration and policy iteration.
- Model-free RL: SARSA, R-max and Q-policy iteration.

The work was a collaboration across several universities. I also helped teach advanced machine learning and reinforcement learning to graduate students.
